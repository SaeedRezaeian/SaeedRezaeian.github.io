Dexterous Telescopic Tendon-Driven Needle Robot

NitinolSeries Elastic Actuation Follow-the-LeaderNeurosurgery

Objective: a meso-scale, multi-stage robotic needle capable of Follow-the-Leader (FTL) navigation to minimize tissue damage during neurosurgery.

Technology: a concentric-tube manipulator built from notched superelastic nitinol tubes, driven by Series Elastic Actuators (SEAs) for precise force control.

Impact: sub-millimeter precision with a tip position error of 0.92 mm, and validated “S” and “C” shape maneuvers with ~1.1 mm FTL tracking error.

My role: mechanical design & prototyping · software & control · kinematic modeling and design constraints · experimental validation

MRI-Guided Robotic System for Intracranial Therapeutic Delivery

MR-CompatiblePiezoelectric Actuation Tendon-DrivenCAR-T Delivery

Objective: a meso-scale robotic system for minimally invasive delivery of immunotherapy agents (e.g., CAR-T cells) into brain tumors.

Technology: a tendon-driven telescopic needle robot using superelastic nitinol tubes and non-magnetic piezoelectric actuators.

Impact: significant tumor coverage and high-precision steering validated under live 1.5T MRI guidance.

My role: mechanical design & prototyping · system integration · kinematics and mechanical modeling · characterization and validation tests

Steerable Laser Interstitial Thermotherapy (SLIT) Robot

U.S. PatentHydraulic Actuation Laser AblationMotion Planning

Objective: an MR-compatible steerable robotic probe for laser interstitial thermal therapy (LITT), reaching large, irregular brain tumors that straight probes cannot.

Technology: a 1.47 mm superelastic nitinol steerable needle with hydraulic actuation; the tip integrates a custom PDMS/carbon-nanoparticle mixture that converts laser energy into localized thermal ablation, with an optimization algorithm that plans motion and ablation centers from MRI data.

Impact: validated through FEA and in-vitro multi-site ablation tests, achieving consistent oval heating zones and sub-millimeter steering accuracy — while reducing required laser power from 14 W to 0.7 W.

My role: mechanical design & prototyping · motion planning & optimization · system characterization and ablation testing

Control System Design for Lower-Limb Prostheses

Impedance ControlsEMG System IdentificationMachine Learning

Objective: AI-based locomotion-mode recognition from surface EMG for an ankle prosthesis controller, and impedance control of a knee prosthesis with frictional torque estimation.

Technology: sEMG sensors on lower-limb muscles for gait-phase detection; a hierarchical impedance control scheme with fuzzy-logic friction compensation and least-squares system identification.

Impact: reliable discrimination of walking speeds and ramp ascent for automatic gait-mode switching, reduced peak motor current during direction reversal, and significantly improved trajectory tracking by neutralizing internal friction.

My role: data acquisition & processing · machine-learning models · control system design · experimental validation
